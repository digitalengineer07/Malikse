## Export checklist

- Recommended working size: 1080 × 1920 portrait, PNG or JPEG. Keep text legible.
- Google's phone screenshot requirements include at least two screenshots, dimensions 320–3840 px, and longest dimension no more than twice the shortest. Check current Console rules for your supported devices.
- If tablet or other form factors are supported, capture those layouts as required by Console rather than enlarging phone images.
- Store icon: prepare a 512 × 512 PNG from the approved app icon.
- Feature graphic: prepare a 1024 × 500 JPG or 24-bit PNG without alpha. Suggested copy: “MalikSe” / “Explore properties. Connect with your assigned team.”
- Avoid performance rankings, download counts, fabricated reviews, investment-return promises and claims of government endorsement.
- Do not crop out failures to imply a feature works. Remove sensitive fixtures at the data source, not by disguising production user data.

Official asset guidance: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151
