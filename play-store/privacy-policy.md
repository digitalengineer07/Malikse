## Information we handle

**Account information.** When you register or manage an account, we handle your name, email address and/or mobile number, account identifier, authentication information, verification status and account preferences. Password-based accounts use a password hash on our server.

**Identity verification.** If you use features requiring KYC, we collect the identity documents you submit, information contained in those documents, your verification status and review records. Documents may contain your photograph, address, date of birth or government-issued identification information, depending on what you upload. These documents are not intended for public listing display.

**Property information.** We collect listing details, property location, photographs, any supported video uploads, ownership and other supporting documents, assignment information and review history. Information selected for a published listing can be visible to other users and guests. Supporting documents may be made available through controlled access where the service permits it; do not assume every uploaded property document is public or every document is private.

**Offers and conversations.** We handle offer amounts, terms, messages, timestamps and deal status. Buyer offers and conversations go to the Advisor or Verifier assigned to the property. Buyer chat is not a direct chat with the property owner.

**Activity and preferences.** We handle property views, searches, saved properties and search preferences to operate discovery, trending lists and recommendations.

**Location.** If you choose a location-based feature and grant permission, we access device location to help select a property location. You may also supply location information manually. Property coordinates submitted in a listing are stored with that listing. Map and place-search providers may receive requests associated with the areas you view or search.

**Device and service information.** Where notifications are enabled, we store a push token, platform and account association to deliver updates. Service providers may process network information such as IP addresses and technical logs when supplying hosting, authentication, maps, email and notifications. We use information to create and maintain accounts; authenticate users; review identity and property submissions; display eligible listings; save preferences; personalize discovery; route offers and messages to the assigned team; track deal progress; send service updates; respond to support requests; and investigate misuse or resolve disputes.
