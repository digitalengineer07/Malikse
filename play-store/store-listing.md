# Store listing — English (India)

## App name

MalikSe: Property Search

## Short description

Find verified properties, save favourites and chat with your assigned team.

## Full description

Find your next property with MalikSe. Browse verified, available listings without creating an account, explore locations on the map, and compare property details before taking the next step.

DISCOVER PROPERTIES
Explore trending listings and newly added properties. Sign in for recommendations informed by your searches and wishlist. Search locations and filter properties to find options that suit your needs.

SAVE YOUR FAVOURITES
Create an account to keep properties in your wishlist and return to the listings that interest you.

MAKE OFFERS WITH SUPPORT
After your identity verification is approved, submit an offer on an available property. Your offer and buyer conversations are handled by the Advisor or Verifier assigned to that property, helping you follow the discussion in one place.

LIST YOUR PROPERTY
Complete identity verification, enter your property details, and upload photos and supporting documents. Provide an Advisor ID when you have one; otherwise, your submission is assigned to a Verifier. Follow review progress and respond to correction requests from your account.

FOLLOW YOUR PROGRESS
Keep track of your listings, offers and conversations. Once the assigned team confirms and closes a completed deal, the property is marked sold and is no longer available for purchase through the marketplace.

Browsing is available without signing in. Saving properties and other account features require sign-in. Posting properties and making offers require approved identity verification. Available listings and services vary by location.

Property verification describes MalikSe's listing review process. It does not replace independent legal checks or guarantee title, price, availability or investment returns.
